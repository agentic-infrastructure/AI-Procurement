## Company

## Process / Lifecycle
1. **Vendor qualification:** discovery → prequalification (as part of or before RFI) → qualification (as part of RFP) → approved vendor list (AVL determines who advances to RFQ).
2. **RFx:** RFI (broad information gathering) → RFP (focused; general or direct project information; awards may be made at this stage in Agentic's early years) → RFQ (AVL vendors). Bid evaluation at each RFx stage; award normally made in the RFQ stage.
3. **PO process:** to be determined; to be built with AI.
4. **Contract management:** Project Manager and Procurement Lead manage the contract to success, assisted by the Project Engineer. Legal, development, and corporate functions consulted as needed.
